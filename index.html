Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver outside Lodwar?
Yes. We are based in Lodwar, Turkana County, and deliver to organizations across Turkana County and to other counties in Kenya. Delivery cost and lead time depend on the item and the destination, and both are stated in your quotation before you commit to anything.
How quickly can you provide a quotation?
For standard catalogue items we aim to send a written quotation within one working day of receiving a clear request. Items that need special sourcing or import take longer — we tell you the expected timeline when we acknowledge your request, rather than leaving you to chase it.
Are government charges included in the listed fees?
No. The listed KSh 1,000 and KSh 5,000 amounts are Dickory’s service fees only. Any fee, levy or invoice issued by a government authority is separate and is paid by the client directly to that authority. Where an authority issues its own payment instruction, we tell you the amount and where to pay it.
What documents are needed for each registration?

AGPO: current-calendar-year CR12 (or CR13 for partnerships), certificate of incorporation, IDs of directors and shareholders, passport photo, KRA tax compliance certificate in the company name, and evidence of at least 70% qualifying ownership.

IFMIS: company email, contact first and last name, phone area code and mobile number, KRA PIN certificate, certificate of incorporation, and business permit.

eGP: BRS registration number, Kenya national ID number, KRA PIN, registration/incorporation or compliance certificate, and an official phone number and email address.

Work permits and visitor’s pass: the checklist depends on the permit class and your circumstances — we send the exact list once we have confirmed your category. Each service page has the full detail.

Can you source items not listed on the website?
Yes. The categories shown are examples of what we regularly supply, not a closed catalogue. Send us the specification, quantity and delivery location for anything else and we will confirm whether we can source it and at what price.
