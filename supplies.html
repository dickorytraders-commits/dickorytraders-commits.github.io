Supply categories

What we supply, and what to send us

Each category below lists the goods we are asked for most often. They are examples, not a closed catalogue — if what you need is not here, send the specification anyway and we will confirm whether we can source it.

01

Electronics & ICT Equipment

Laptops, desktops, printers, monitors, networking equipment and accessories — specified to the model and configuration your team actually uses.

  • Laptops, desktops and tablets
  • Printers, scanners and toner/ink consumables
  • Monitors, projectors and display equipment
  • Routers, switches, cabling and networking accessories
  • External drives, UPS units and power accessories
  • Keyboards, mice and peripheral accessories

Tell us the model or the minimum specification (processor, RAM, storage, screen size), the quantity, and whether you need warranties or installation. Where an exact model is unavailable we propose the closest equivalent and say so before quoting.

02

Office Furniture & Fittings

Desks, chairs, storage and boardroom furniture for offices, field stations and institutional buildings.

  • Desks, workstations and executive tables
  • Office chairs, visitor seating and benches
  • Filing cabinets, cupboards and shelving
  • Meeting and boardroom tables
  • Reception and waiting-area furniture
  • Partitions, whiteboards and fittings

Send the room or the item list, the quantity of each, and any size or finish requirement. For a new office, a simple floor plan or a list of rooms is enough for us to propose a layout and a cost.

03

Office Supplies & Stationery

The recurring consumables that keep an office running, supplied in the quantities you actually use.

  • Paper, notebooks, files and folders
  • Pens, markers, pencils and desk accessories
  • Toner, ink cartridges and printer paper
  • Staplers, punches, scissors and tape
  • Filing, archiving and binding materials
  • Envelopes, labels and mailing supplies

A simple list with quantities is enough. For recurring needs, tell us your monthly or quarterly usage and we can quote a standing supply arrangement instead of one-off orders.

04

Electrical & General Equipment

Electrical fittings, lighting, small tools and power equipment for offices, institutions and field sites.

  • Cables, sockets, switches and conduits
  • Lighting, bulbs and solar accessories
  • Small tools and hardware
  • Generators and power accessories
  • Extension leads, adaptors and stabilisers
  • Safety equipment and protective gear

State the rating or specification where it matters (voltage, wattage, cable gauge) and whether the items must meet a particular standard. For site work, tell us the location so we can account for transport.

Cleaning, Hygiene & Institutional Supplies

Cleaning chemicals, protective wear and hygiene consumables for offices, schools, clinics and institutions.

  • Detergents, disinfectants and cleaning chemicals
  • Gloves, masks, aprons and protective wear
  • Tissue, soap, sanitiser and dispensers
  • Brooms, mops, buckets and dustbins
  • Waste bags and sanitation supplies
  • Institutional hygiene consumables

Tell us the number of staff or the size of the facility, and whether you want a one-off delivery or a recurring monthly supply. Bulk quantities are usually cheaper per unit and we will say so.

How supply works

Five steps from request to delivery

Delivery cost is quoted upfront and never added afterwards.

01Understand

Items, specification, quantity, delivery location and deadline.

02Source

We confirm availability with suppliers before we price anything.

03Quote

04Deliver

Delivery to your site, with the paperwork you need to receive it.

05Follow through

We confirm receipt and resolve any shortfall after delivery.

Before you write

What makes a quotation accurate

Five details decide whether a quotation is right the first time. Send what you have — we will ask for the rest.

1. Item or serviceWhat you need, with any specification, brand or model.
2. QuantityHow many units, or the scope of the service.
3. Delivery locationTown, site or office where it should be delivered.
4. Required deadlineThe date you need it by, or your delivery window.
5. Budget rangeIf you have one — it helps us propose the right option.
