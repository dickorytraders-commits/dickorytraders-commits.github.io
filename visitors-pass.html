Visitor’s Pass Extension

Support extending a visitor’s pass for the additional period you need, submitted before your current pass expires.

KSh 1,000 Dickory service fee

If you are in Kenya on a visitor’s pass and need more time, the pass has to be extended before it expires. We prepare and submit the extension application for the additional period you are seeking.

Who qualifies

  • Visitors currently in Kenya on a valid visitor’s pass who need to stay longer.
  • Applicants whose reason for the extended stay is consistent with visitor status — tourism, family visit, short-term business meetings or similar.
  • The applicable maximum stay is considered as part of the process; we confirm what is realistic for your situation before you pay.

Documents and details you need to provide

  • Passport bio-data page and the current visitor’s pass.
  • Passport-size photograph.
  • The additional period you are requesting, and the reason for the extended stay.
  • Evidence of accommodation or host details for the extended period.
  • Return or onward travel details where the authority requires them.

What the KSh 1,000 fee covers

Included

  • Confirming the extension period that can realistically be requested
  • Issuing the document checklist
  • Preparing and submitting the extension application
  • Following up and updating you until a decision is issued

Not included

  • Government extension fees or any invoice issued by the authority
  • Obtaining photographs, accommodation evidence or travel documents
  • Applications made after a pass has already expired

Expected timeline

Timing is set by the authority and depends on the period requested and the completeness of the file. We confirm the expected timeline at intake. Apply before your current pass expires — an application made after expiry is a different, harder process.

Next step

Tell us the additional period you need and the reason for the extended stay, and send your passport bio-data page and current pass. We will confirm what can be requested before you pay.
